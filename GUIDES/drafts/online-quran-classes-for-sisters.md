## What sisters study online

The curriculum is the same complete track, personalized to your starting point:

- **Noorani Qaida** — for sisters starting from zero (including reverts and sisters who never learned as children). Arabic alphabet, joining letters, basic reading rules.
- **Nazra Quran** — fluent reading of the full Quran, page by page, with correction.
- **Tajweed** — proper pronunciation and recitation rules, taught one-to-one so every mistake is heard and fixed.

Your teacher assesses your level in the first lesson and builds the plan from there — a sister who reads Arabic already skips straight past Qaida.

## How to choose the right academy (a sister's checklist)

Not every academy that advertises "sisters' classes" delivers the same thing. Ask these before you enroll:

1. **Can I specifically request a female teacher — guaranteed, not "if available"?** Get this in writing. "We try our best" is not a guarantee.
2. **Are lessons truly one-to-one, or group video calls?** Group video lessons mean other students can see and hear you. For sisters, one-to-one is the privacy standard.
3. **What are the teachers' qualifications?** Ask where they studied and what they teach — a teacher handling Tajweed should know Tajweed deeply, not just basic reading.
4. **How flexible is scheduling — and rescheduling?** Family life is unpredictable. Can you move a class without penalty? Can you pause during travel or Ramadan schedule changes?
5. **What does the trial include?** A real trial is actual lessons with a real teacher — not a demo video or a sales call. Ours is three full one-to-one classes.
6. **Is there a clear way to change teachers?** Fit matters. A good academy switches your teacher on request, no awkwardness, no charge.

Red flags: no trial, vague answers about teacher qualifications, pressure to pay for months upfront, or classes where you can't confirm the teacher is female before paying.

## Common worries, answered honestly

**"I'm a complete beginner as an adult — is it too late?"**
No. A large share of our sister students are adults starting from zero — including reverts. Adult beginners often progress faster than children because they practice deliberately. The first lesson is always the hardest; after that it's just the next lesson.

**"I can only do odd hours."**
That's normal, not a problem. Sisters' classes are the most schedule-flexible we offer precisely because of this — early mornings, school hours, late evenings. Tell the academy your window; a real academy builds around it.

**"What if my internet or device is basic?"**
One-to-one video classes work on a phone with a normal connection. You don't need a laptop or fast broadband — a smartphone and a quiet corner are enough.

**"How long until I can read fluently?"**
It depends on your starting point and consistency, but sisters attending 3–4 classes a week typically complete Noorani Qaida foundations in a few months and move into fluent Nazra reading after that. Anyone promising exact weeks is guessing — consistency beats speed claims.

**"Can my daughter and I learn together?"**
Many academies offer family arrangements — separate one-to-one lessons, often back-to-back, sometimes with a family discount. Ask; it's a common request.

## FAQs

**Are online Quran classes for sisters as effective as in-person?**
For one-to-one learning, yes — often more effective. The teacher's full attention is on your recitation, every mistake is corrected in the moment, and screen sharing lets the teacher point to exactly what you're reading. No commute, no missed classes.

**Can I learn Tajweed properly online?**
Yes — Tajweed is actually well-suited to online one-to-one teaching because correction is entirely about listening: the teacher hears your pronunciation, demonstrates the correct Makhraj (pronunciation point), and has you repeat until it's right. That's identical online and in person.

**Do I need to know Arabic first?**
No. Noorani Qaida assumes zero knowledge — that's the starting point for most adult beginner sisters.

**What if I want to memorize (Hifz)?**
Hifz programs for sisters run fully online with structured daily targets and revision cycles. One-to-one Hifz works well because the teacher listens to your entire sabaq (new lesson) every session — nothing slips through.

**Is there an option for my young daughter too?**
Yes — kids' classes run on the same platform, and many mothers schedule their own lesson right after their daughter's. Female teachers are available for girls as well.
